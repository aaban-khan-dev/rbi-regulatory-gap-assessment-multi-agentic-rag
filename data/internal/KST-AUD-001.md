## 1. Purpose

1.1 This charter defines the independent assurance Kestrel obtains over its technology: what is audited, by whom, how often, and where the results go.

## 2. Independence

2.1 Internal Audit reports functionally to the Audit Committee and administratively to the CEO. No auditor assesses work they performed or designed in the previous two years.

## 3. Annual external payment-system audit

3.1 Once in every April-to-March cycle, Internal Audit commissions an external firm to audit the payment platform end to end. The audit covers governance, change management, access control, operations, business continuity, and the security of the wallet, card and UPI channels.

3.2 The same engagement includes a cybersecurity assessment: external and internal penetration testing, a configuration review of the cloud estate, and a review of the SOC's detection coverage.

3.3 On the day the engagement is signed, the firm must be on CERT-In's current panel of approved auditing organisations. Internal Audit checks the panel and files the evidence before signing the engagement contract.

3.4 Kestrel's accounts run from April to March. The signed audit opinion, with management responses, goes to the RBI DPSS regional office no later than 31 May. The Audit Committee sees it before it is sent.

## 4. Source code assurance

4.1 Code for Ledger Core, Sentinel and Onboard is reviewed by an independent application security firm before each major release.

4.2 For third-party software Kestrel does not own (the card processor module and the V-CIP video platform), Kestrel obtains a written assurance from the vendor every twelve months that the delivered code contains no known malicious or covert functionality.

## 5. Internal audit plan

5.1 Internal Audit runs a risk-based annual plan approved by the Audit Committee. Technology audits in the plan include access recertification, Rules Console change control, TreasuryDesk reconciliation logic and vendor management.

5.2 Findings are rated High, Medium or Low. High findings are reported to the Audit Committee at its next meeting; all findings are tracked to closure in the audit tracker.

## 6. Regulator access

6.1 Kestrel gives RBI and its appointed auditors access to systems, records and staff on request, covering systems hosted by our vendors too.
