## 1. Our commitment

1.1 Every customer who is unhappy with KestrelPay should be able to tell us easily, get an answer quickly, and know what to do if they are still not satisfied. This policy sets how we do that. It is published on our website and in the app in all ten app languages, including English and Hindi.

## 2. Ways to reach us

2.1 **In the app:** Help, then Raise a concern, with categories for failed payments, top-ups, cash-out, card issues, fraud and account access.

2.2 **Phone:** the CareDesk helpline, 8:00 to 22:00 every day. A separate fraud line runs 24x7 (see KST-CX-004).

2.3 **Email and post:** to the addresses shown on the website, in the app and on the back of every KestrelPay Card.

2.4 **At outlets:** every Kestrel Points outlet displays the CareDesk number and the escalation contacts.

2.5 The website and app list every active Kestrel Points outlet with its outlet code, address and phone number.

## 3. Handling a concern

3.1 CareDesk creates a ticket for every concern, whatever the channel, and sends the customer the ticket number by SMS straight away. Customers can track the ticket status in the app or by quoting the number on the helpline.

3.2 **Turnaround.** A CareDesk agent starts work on the ticket within 48 hours. Our target is full resolution within 7 days, and no ticket may stay open beyond 30 days from the day we received it.

3.3 Failed-transaction tickets follow the turnaround and compensation timelines set by RBI for authorised payment systems. CareDesk calculates any compensation due automatically and credits it without the customer having to ask.

3.4 Disputes involving UPI or card transactions follow the dispute rules of NPCI and the card network, using their online dispute systems.

## 4. Escalation

4.1 **Level 1:** CareDesk agent.

4.2 **Level 2:** Grievance Officer. Customers not satisfied after 7 days, or with a Level 1 response, can escalate in the app.

4.3 **Level 3:** Nodal Officer. Named on the website with direct phone and email.

4.4 **Outside Kestrel:** if a complaint is still unresolved to the customer's satisfaction after 30 days, or is unhappy with our final reply, they can approach the RBI Integrated Ombudsman. Every final reply says so and includes the Ombudsman's complaint portal link.

## 5. Customer education

5.1 The Help centre has FAQs on every product, fees, limits, and staying safe. It explains how to keep the PIN secret and what to do if the phone is lost or a payment looks wrong.

## 6. Monitoring

6.1 CareDesk produces a monthly complaints MIS covering volumes by category and channel, turnaround performance against section 3, escalations, and the top root causes. It goes to the Head of Customer Experience and the COO.

6.2 Root causes that recur in three consecutive months are raised to the relevant product owner as fix tickets.

## 7. Review

7.1 This policy is reviewed by the Board each year.
