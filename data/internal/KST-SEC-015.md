# Brand Protection and Anti-Phishing Procedure

## 1. Purpose

1.1 Fraudsters copy the KestrelPay name and look to trick customers into installing fake apps or entering their PIN on fake websites. This procedure sets how Kestrel finds these imitations and gets them removed.

1.2 It implements paragraph 29 of the RBI Master Direction on Digital Payment Security Controls (2021), which Kestrel adopted as its benchmark for monitoring imitation apps and sites.

## 2. Ownership

2.1 Brand Protection is a two-person team in the Security Governance function. It runs all monitoring and takedown work in-house.

2.2 The 2023 cost review compared in-house monitoring with commercial takedown vendors and concluded that in-house coverage meets the paragraph 29 benchmark. No external monitoring or takedown vendor is engaged.

## 3. What we monitor

3.1 **App stores.** The team searches Google Play, the Apple App Store and the top five third-party APK sites daily for apps using "Kestrel", "KestrelPay" or the logo, using a maintained keyword list.

3.2 **Lookalike domains.** Registrar alert subscriptions, set up by the team, flag new domains containing our brand terms or common misspellings of them.

3.3 **Social media.** The team reviews brand-mention searches on the main social platforms each working day, looking for fake customer-care handles.

3.4 **Customer reports.** CareDesk tags any customer report of a fake app, site or caller and routes it to Brand Protection.

## 4. Takedown

4.1 When an imitation is confirmed, the team files a takedown request directly with the app store, registrar, hosting provider or social platform using their abuse forms, attaching trademark registration evidence.

4.2 Takedown requests are tracked in a shared tracker. Requests open for more than 7 days are escalated to Legal.

4.3 If an imitation is actively harvesting credentials, the team asks Fraud Operations to add the related domains and device fingerprints to Sentinel's block lists.

## 5. Customer warnings

5.1 Confirmed campaigns are announced to customers through in-app banners and SMS, with a reminder that Kestrel never asks for the PIN or one-time code.

## 6. Reporting

6.1 Brand Protection reports the monthly count of imitations found and removed to the CISO.
