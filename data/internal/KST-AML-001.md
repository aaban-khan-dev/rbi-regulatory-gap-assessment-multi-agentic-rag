## 1. Purpose

1.1 This is Kestrel's Board-approved policy for knowing our customers and preventing the KestrelPay platform from being used to launder money or finance terrorism. The procedures that implement it are KST-AML-004 (onboarding), KST-AML-006 (KYC refresh) and KST-AML-009 (sanctions screening).

## 2. Accountability

2.1 **Designated Director:** the Whole-Time Director (Operations), nominated by the Board. Their name and contact details have been filed with FIU-IND and RBI.

2.2 **Principal Officer:** the Head of Financial Crime Compliance (FCC). They own transaction monitoring, suspicious-activity reporting and liaison with FIU-IND. They are never the same person as the Designated Director.

2.3 **Senior management for KYC purposes:** the CEO, COO, Chief Risk Officer and Chief Compliance Officer.

2.4 Internal Audit tests compliance with this policy every quarter, and its notes go to the Audit Committee.

## 3. Enterprise ML/TF risk assessment

3.1 FCC runs an enterprise-wide ML/TF risk assessment covering customers, geographies, products and channels. It also folds in any sector typologies that supervisors share with us.

3.2 The Risk Committee of the Board sets how often the assessment runs. The current setting is every April, plus an extra run before any new product or channel goes live. The gap between two runs may never exceed twelve months.

3.3 Each run produces a written report scored against Kestrel's size and footprint, with a mitigation plan. The report goes to the Risk Committee, and a copy is kept in the supervisory evidence file so it can be handed to an inspector on request.

## 4. Who we accept

4.1 **Inclusion.** Our acceptance rules must not shut out people on low incomes, first-time digital users or others who are underserved by the banking system. Product design should keep onboarding open to them, for example through the Lite wallet, which needs only minimal details.

4.2 **Who we decline.** We do not open wallets in false, anonymous or borrowed names. We do not open a wallet when we cannot complete our identity checks.

4.3 Applications that fail any automated rule in Onboard are declined automatically. The applicant sees a standard "We could not verify your details" message and may apply again after 30 days.

4.4 Customers already holding a verified Plus wallet do not repeat identity verification to add the KestrelPay Card; their customer ID (UCIC) carries the verification across.

## 5. Customer risk rating

5.1 Every customer is placed in one of three bands, L, M or H. The Customer Risk Rating model in Onboard assigns the band from occupation and declared income, geography, product held, onboarding channel, transaction behaviour and screening results.

5.2 The band and the reasons for it are internal. Customer-facing teams cannot see them, and they are never disclosed to the customer.

5.3 **Re-scoring.** FCC runs a full portfolio re-score once a year, every April, and reviews band changes for customers moving into band H.

5.4 **Remote onboarding.** Customers onboarded without an in-person or video verification (DigiLocker document pull or CKYCR record download) start in band M and go onto the "New Remote" watch for 180 days, with tighter Sentinel thresholds. After 180 days they return to normal monitoring.

5.5 Customers verified by video (V-CIP) are treated the same as customers verified in person.

## 6. Politically exposed persons

6.1 Watchlist Hub checks every applicant, and every customer nightly, against our PEP data set. The data set includes family members and known close associates.

6.2 Opening a wallet for a PEP, or keeping a wallet when an existing customer becomes a PEP, needs written sign-off from the Chief Compliance Officer or the CEO.

6.3 Before sign-off, FCC records where the customer's money and wealth come from, with evidence (salary slips, income-tax returns, business records).

6.4 PEP wallets stay in band H for as long as the relationship lasts, and FCC reviews their transactions every month.

## 7. Transaction monitoring and reporting

7.1 The AML monitoring module raises alerts when activity does not fit the customer's band and profile. FCC analysts work the alerts within the timelines in the FCC operating manual.

7.2 The Principal Officer files Suspicious Transaction Reports and the other prescribed reports with FIU-IND within the statutory timelines. The fact that a report has been filed is never disclosed to the customer.

7.3 Filing a report does not, by itself, lead to restrictions on the customer's wallet.

## 8. Records and confidentiality

8.1 Record retention follows KST-DAT-002.

8.2 Customer information is used only to provide the service. It is never used for cross-selling third-party products without the customer's explicit consent.

## 9. Staff

9.1 New joiners are screened before hiring, and every employee completes the annual AML e-learning module.
