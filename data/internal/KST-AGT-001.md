## 1. Purpose

1.1 Kestrel Points are independent retail outlets (kirana stores, mobile recharge shops, pharmacies) contracted to add cash to KestrelPay Plus wallets and pay out cash to customers. This policy sets how outlets are selected, contracted, supervised and exited.

1.2 Kestrel remains answerable to customers and to the regulator for everything an outlet does in our name, including how it handles customer data and cash. Outlet owners are contractors, not employees, but our controls treat their actions as our own.

## 2. Roles

2.1 **Agent Network Management (ANM)** owns outlet recruitment, contracting, the outlet scorecard and exits.

2.2 **Area Managers** (one per cluster of roughly 250 outlets) carry out field visits and first-line conduct checks.

2.3 **Financial Crime Compliance (FCC)** sets the outlet vetting standard and reviews outlets flagged for suspicious patterns.

## 3. Selecting and vetting outlets

3.1 Before an outlet is activated, ANM collects the owner's identity documents, PAN and a shop establishment or GST registration, runs the owner through Watchlist Hub, and obtains a police verification certificate.

3.2 An Area Manager visits the premises before activation and records geo-tagged photographs of the shopfront and cash counter in the Agent Portal.

3.3 Outlets must hold a working smartphone running the Kestrel Agent app and a printer or SMS-receipt facility.

## 4. What outlets may and may not do

4.1 Outlets may add cash to Plus wallets and pay out cash from Plus wallets, within the caps in the Wallet Product and Limits Policy (KST-PRD-001). The Agent Portal enforces the agent-channel cash-out limit of ₹2,000 per withdrawal and ₹10,000 per wallet per calendar month.

4.2 Outlets may not open wallets, change customer details, or accept cash for Lite wallets.

4.3 Outlets may not charge customers anything beyond the published Kestrel fee. Every outlet contract contains an explicit prohibition on side charges, and breach is grounds for immediate termination.

4.4 Every cash transaction generates a receipt, printed or by SMS, in Kestrel's name.

4.5 Outlets display the Kestrel Points signboard, the fee chart and the CareDesk helpline number at the counter.

## 5. Supervision and performance review

5.1 ANM maintains a live scorecard for every active outlet in the Agent Portal. It covers transaction volumes, cash-handling exceptions, customer complaints logged against the outlet in CareDesk, and Sentinel alerts tied to the outlet's terminal.

5.2 Every active outlet, regardless of size, receives a full performance and conduct review at least once every twelve months. The review is recorded in the Agent Portal and signed off by the Area Manager and an ANM reviewer.

5.3 In addition, Area Managers make unannounced visits to a rolling sample of outlets every quarter, weighted towards outlets with complaints or alerts.

5.4 Outlets rated Red on the scorecard are suspended within 24 hours pending remediation. Outlets rated Red in two consecutive reviews are exited.

## 6. Customer data at outlets

6.1 Outlets never see full wallet numbers or balances; the Agent app shows masked identifiers only.

6.2 Paper records of any kind are prohibited at outlets. Outlet contracts require confidentiality of anything the owner learns about customers, surviving termination.

## 7. Training

7.1 Outlet owners complete Kestrel's onboarding training before activation and a refresher module every year, covering cash handling, fraud red flags and customer conduct.

## 8. Exit

8.1 On exit, the outlet's terminal credentials are revoked the same day and the signboard is recovered within 30 days.
