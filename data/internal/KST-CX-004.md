## 1. Purpose

1.1 This policy tells customers what happens when money leaves their wallet without their permission: how to tell us, how quickly we act, and how much, if anything, they bear. A summary is given to every customer when they open a wallet, and the full policy is published in the app and on the website.

## 2. Channels

2.1 Statements in this policy apply to remote payments (app, UPI, online card use) and to in-person payments (ATM, point of sale, agent cash-out).

## 3. Telling us

3.1 Customers must keep SMS alerts switched on; email alerts are optional. Every debit alert carries the fraud line number and a link to report the transaction.

3.2 The fraud line, the in-app "This wasn't me" button, the website form and the reporting email all work 24x7.

3.3 The moment a report arrives, CareDesk sends an automatic acknowledgement with a case number, and Sentinel blocks further debits from the wallet. Notify records the time each alert was delivered and the time each customer report was received.

3.4 We encourage customers to report as soon as they notice anything. The longer the delay, the harder it is to stop the money moving on.

## 4. How much the customer bears

| Situation | Customer bears |
|---|---|
| Loss caused by Kestrel's own fraud, negligence or deficiency, whether or not reported | Nothing |
| Breach elsewhere in the system (not Kestrel, not the customer), reported within 3 days of the alert | Nothing |
| Same, reported on day 4 to day 7 | The transaction amount or ₹10,000, whichever is less |
| Same, reported after day 7 | Decided case by case by the Disputes Committee, capped at the transaction amount |
| Customer shared PIN or one-time code | The full loss up to the time of reporting; Kestrel bears anything after the report |

4.1 Days are counted from the day after the alert was delivered.

4.2 The Disputes Committee may waive any customer share, including in negligence cases.

4.3 If Kestrel says a customer was negligent, Kestrel must prove it; the burden never sits with the customer.

## 5. Timelines

5.1 **Provisional credit.** Within 10 days of the report, Disputes credits the disputed amount back to the wallet, dated as of the original debit, even if this takes the balance above its normal ceiling. We do not wait for any insurance claim.

5.2 **Final outcome.** Every disputed-debit case in CareDesk carries a 90-day clock, counted from the day the case was logged. The Disputes team aims to finish the investigation within 30 days.

5.3 If the 90-day clock runs out and the case is still open, or Disputes cannot decide who was at fault, the system settles the case automatically. The customer is paid what the table in section 4 gives them on a no-fault basis, and the provisional credit is made permanent.

## 6. Disputes Committee

6.1 The Disputes Committee (Head of CX, Head of Fraud Operations, Legal) meets weekly to decide cases outside the standard rules.

## 7. Review

7.1 The Board approves this policy and reviews it annually.
