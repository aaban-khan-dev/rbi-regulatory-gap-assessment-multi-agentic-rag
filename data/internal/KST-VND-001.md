## 1. Purpose

1.1 Kestrel relies on outside firms for parts of its service. This policy sets how those firms are chosen, contracted, monitored and replaced, so that using a vendor never weakens our obligations to customers or regulators.

## 2. Tiering

2.1 **Tier 1 (material):** vendors whose failure would stop payments, expose customer data, or affect a regulated activity.

2.2 **Tier 2:** vendors with access to internal data but not customer data.

2.3 **Tier 3:** everyone else.

2.4 Current Tier 1 vendors:

| Vendor | Service |
|---|---|
| Cloud provider | Hosting for all production systems (two Indian regions) |
| VeriDoc | Document authenticity checks, face match and liveness checks for onboarding |
| V-CIP video platform | Video verification software used by Kestrel KYC officers |
| Managed SOC partner | 24x7 security monitoring |
| Card processor | KestrelPay Card issuing and authorisation |
| SMS gateway | Delivery of one-time codes and alerts |

## 3. Before signing

3.1 Every Tier 1 vendor goes through financial, security, data-protection and business-continuity due diligence. The Third-Party Risk team signs it off before contract.

3.2 Outsourcing a new activity to a Tier 1 vendor needs Risk Committee approval.

## 4. Contract terms

4.1 Every Tier 1 contract must include:
- the right for Kestrel, RBI and auditors appointed by either to audit and inspect the vendor's premises, systems and records, wherever they are hosted;
- storage of Kestrel payment data only in India;
- notice to Kestrel within 6 hours of any security breach affecting Kestrel systems, data or processes;
- service levels, with service credits for misses;
- confidentiality obligations that survive termination;
- an exit plan and Kestrel's right to terminate for regulatory reasons;
- subcontracting only with Kestrel's prior written consent.

## 5. Ongoing oversight

5.1 Each Tier 1 vendor has a named relationship owner, who reviews service performance monthly.

5.2 The Third-Party Risk team reassesses each Tier 1 vendor's security and continuity controls every year, including an on-site or remote audit.

5.3 Tier 1 vendor issues are reported to the Risk Committee each quarter.

## 6. Exit

6.1 Every Tier 1 service has a tested exit plan naming an alternative provider or an in-house fallback.
